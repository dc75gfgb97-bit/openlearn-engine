# Proposed evaluation protocol — not completed research

## Question and hypothesis

Can an interactive narrative with explanatory feedback help adult learners distinguish sampling bias and causal uncertainty? A possible hypothesis is improvement on unseen evidence-reasoning questions after the activity. The current demo provides practice only and does not establish improvement.

## Stage 1: formative usability

Recruit a small voluntary adult convenience sample, for example 5–8 participants, after appropriate consent and any required institutional review. Explain AI assistance and that this is not a graded assessment. Ask participants to complete the story, describe their reasoning and load a sample story. Record task completion, navigation problems and optional anonymous feedback. Avoid recording identifiable information by default. Accommodate keyboard and screen-reader use. Do not present this sample as representative.

## Stage 2: exploratory learning study

Before recruitment, specify the target population, primary outcome, sample-size rationale and analysis plan. Use parallel pre/post questions assessing transfer rather than repeating the demo answers. If feasible, randomise participants between the story and a reading condition with equivalent content and time; avoid conflating narrative effects with additional feedback or exposure. Record missing data and attrition. Seek ethics approval where applicable; participation must not affect assessment or services.

Report effect sizes and uncertainty alongside descriptive results, anonymised qualitative themes and all limitations. Small convenience samples cannot support broad causal claims. Do not publish identifiable raw responses. Define retention, deletion and withdrawal procedures before collecting any data. This prototype currently exports no study data and collects no telemetry; any study instrumentation requires separate consent and implementation.

## Evidence log template

Date / prototype commit / recruitment and consent / participant count / task / instrument / observed result / uncertainty / change made / next question.

No entries or findings have been fabricated. Automated software tests verify program behavior, not educational effectiveness. Quiz results are formative first-answer counts and are not a validated scale.
